About this comparison Jev primitives and method guide

The same farmer question is sent through the current GDB method and four Jev experiments.

ChoiceSelects one label from fixed options, such as Same request or Different request.
NoulEstimates how true a statement is from 0% to 100%, such as whether an answer covers the request.
ScoreGrades something against a numeric rubric. Score is available in Jev but is not used by these methods.
Methods
  • Current method: vector retrieval and Gemma; no Jev primitive.
  • Method 1: Jev Choice selects the closest request.
  • Method 2: Jev Choice checks the request and Jev Noul checks answer coverage.
  • Method 3: two Jev Choices check the subject and task; one Jev Noul checks conflict risk.
  • Method 4: one Jev Choice checks the task; four Jev Nouls check evidence, target, coverage and conflict.
Benchmark result: Method 1 performed best for automatic answers. Method 4 found the most reference candidates.
