Coverage % = existing GDB entries ÷ (GDB entries + open gap questions) for that crop/state pair — a true ratio, not just raw gap volume. This distinguishes a large-but-partially-served gap (e.g. 42 gap questions against 40 existing entries, ~49% covered) from a small-but-severe one (e.g. 14 gap questions against just 2 entries, ~13% covered) — something gap volume alone can't tell apart.
